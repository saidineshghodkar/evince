06 / DECODE THIS

Questions,
answered.

Still curious? Send our student desk a signal and we will get back to you.

Who can participate?+

PulseFest welcomes undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and equivalent students from recognized institutions.

Can students from other colleges join?+

Yes. PulseFest is designed as an inter-collegiate fest, so teams from across the country are invited.

How do I register for an event?+

Choose an event above and use its register action. Our team will confirm your entry and share the event brief.

Is accommodation available?+

Limited accommodation guidance is available for outstation participants. Mention it in your inquiry early.

What do winners receive?+

Cash prizes, trophies, certificates and a very good story to tell. The exact prize is shown on each event card.
